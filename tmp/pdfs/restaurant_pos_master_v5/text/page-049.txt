II.11 Security rules
PII is minimized. Payment secrets are masked. Public QR cannot reach
internal APIs directly. Internal admin actions require permission checks.
Audit records must remain immutable. Sensitive provider credentials are
never embedded in code. No plaintext passwords. No financial sensitive
logging.
II.12 KVKK-aware design rules
KVKK is not only a policy topic; it affects the model. Rules: customer PII
must be minimal; retention metadata should exist early; anonymization
support must be modeled; financial/legal records may be retained while
personal data is removed or anonymized where allowed; personal data
deletion should not break invoice/payment integrity.
Schema correspondence:retention_until, anonymization_status,
anonymized_at on customers and customer_accounts — III.18.1, III.18.2.
