PostgreSQL is the authoritative source of truth. SignalR, printer buffers,
external provider callbacks, QR sessions, and UI state are not authoritative.
II.1.2 English codebase
Every domain term in code must be English. Examples: Order, Bill,
BillAllocation, Payment, PaymentAllocation, DailyMenu,
DailyMenuItem, RecipeVersion, ProductionBatch, PortionReservation,
KitchenTicket, PrintJob, MealCardSettlement, CustomerAccount,
FiscalDocument, ReconciliationCase.
II.1.3 No silent destructive actions
The following cannot be silently deleted: financial transactions, fiscal
documents, audit events, order history, production history, backup history,
reconciliation history. Corrections must be represented as new domain
actions.
II.1.4 Immutable historical references
Anything that affects historical calculation must be snapshotted or version-
referenced. Examples: order price snapshot, recipe version reference,
production batch reference, payment allocation reference, fiscal reference,
meal card settlement reference.
II.2 Bounded contexts
II.2.1 Identity & Authorization — Users, roles, permissions, devices,
sessions.
II.2.2 Catalog — Product catalog, categories, modifiers, tax profiles, price
definitions.
II.2.3 Table Management — Tables, zones, seating, transfer, merge,
operational state.
II.2.4 Order — Channel-independent order lifecycle and order item state.
II.2.5 Bill — Customer-facing bill/adisyon, bill splitting, bill closure,
allocations.
II.2.6 Payment — Tendering, payment states, payment allocations, payment
attempts, reversals.
II.2.7 Cash — Cash sessions, cash movements, counts, differences.
II.2.8 Menu — Static catalog to daily menu mapping.
