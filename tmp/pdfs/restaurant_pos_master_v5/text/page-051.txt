The final product is: single-branch restaurant POS, local-first, English

II.15 Exit gate to Database Schema
The database schema must not be written until the following are fixed in
domain terms: order lifecycle; bill closure invariant; payment allocation
model; split bill support; recipe version → production batch immutable
reference; portion reservation lifecycle; QR confirmation policy;
reconciliation case lifecycle; alert lifecycle; print job idempotency; cash
session lifecycle; meal card vs customer account separation; Table lifecycle
(new item); KitchenTicketItem lifecycle(new item).
Once these are stable, the database schema can be written without
redesign churn. As of this master merge, all fourteen items are stable
and reflected in Part III.
