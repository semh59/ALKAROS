Note on current_order_id / current_bill_id: these are deliberate
soft/cache-pointer foreign keys — they denormalize "what's currently open
on this table" for fast UI reads, are allowed to be temporarily stale during a
transfer/merge, and must never be treated as the source of truth for
order/bill ownership. The source of truth is orders.orders.table_id and
billing.bills.table_id. This note replaces the previously inconsistent
(unlabeled) column documentation.
