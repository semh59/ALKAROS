I.24 Inventory Reservation ↔  Order Lifecycle
Order Draft            →  No reservation
Order Submitted        →  Channel-specific validation
Order Accepted         →  Portion reservation
Kitchen Started         →  Reservation becomes committed
Order Completed         →  Consumed
Rejected                →  No final consumption
Cancellation'ın hangi noktada Released, hangi noktada Waste oluşturacağı
kitchen state'e göre belirlenir.

I.26 V1.2 — Payment
Payment tender types:
Cash, BankCard, MealCard, CustomerAccount
SplitPayment is NOT a tender type (corrected per v5 audit — confirmed
closed). It is a composition of multiple payment records and/or payment
allocations belonging to the same Bill.
Examples: Cash + BankCard · BankCard + MealCard · MealCard +
CustomerAccount · three or more tenders in one Bill.
Payment state (canonical: II.5.3):
Initiated, Pending, Approved, Declined, Cancelled, Unknown, 
ReconciliationRequired, Refunded
