RecipeDerived.
Examples: Cola → QuantityTracked; Chicken Meal → PortionTracked +
RecipeDerived; Napkin → Untracked; Tea → QuantityTracked or

II.3.2 Order
The canonical channel-independent sales request. Origins: cashier, waiter,
QR, online provider. Order is not bill, not payment, not fiscal document, not
kitchen ticket.
Responsibilities: capture intent, capture channel, capture customer/table
context, track item states, trigger inventory reservation at the correct state,
trigger kitchen routing, support cancellation paths, preserve price
snapshot.
II.3.3 Bill
Customer-facing financial summary of one or more orders or order parts.
Bill is not payment. Responsibilities: calculate payable amount, support
splits, support allocations, support closure, support re-open/void policy
only if explicitly allowed, represent adisyon/checkout context.
II.3.4 Payment
A tendering event with a specific method and provider. Methods: cash,
bank card, meal card, customer account, split payment as a composition
model, not a separate tender type. Payment is not bill closure by itself.
II.3.5 PaymentAllocation
The mapping between a payment event and the amount assigned to a bill
allocation or payable segment. Exists because: one payment can settle part
of a bill; one bill can be settled by many payments; cash tendered can
exceed allocated amount and generate change; payment callbacks can be
partial or uncertain. Duplicate-protection enforcement: III.8.2.
II.3.6 DailyMenu
The operational menu for a specific business date: what is available today,
how many portions are planned, what price applies today, whether the item
is open/closed/sold out. Not the same as the product catalog.
