II.6.7 Recipe versioning
A production batch must always point to an immutable recipe version.
Rules: a recipe may have many versions; production batch references
exactly one version; historical cost reporting uses the referenced version;
recipe version must not be overwritten in place.
II.6.8 QR ordering
Default policy: restaurant confirmation. Rules: QR submission creates a
pending customer order request; the restaurant may confirm or reject; only
accepted orders reserve portions and proceed operationally; QR must not
expose internal network resources; QR must not be able to create
uncontrolled stock consumption.
II.6.9 Inventory reservation
Rules: reservation is not consumption; available stock cannot become
negative; reservation must be atomic; release returns to availability; waste
is final; restaurant and online channels share the same portion pool.
II.6.10 Print idempotency
Rules: one logical kitchen print request must not generate uncontrolled
duplicates; retry must be idempotent or deduplicated; paper-out or offline
printer must queue the job, not lose the order; print failure is recoverable.
II.6.11 Reconciliation
(Renumbered from the erroneous duplicate "§6.10" in the prior draft —
Corrections v5 §8.1.)
Rules: any mismatch between authoritative states generates a
reconciliation case; mismatches are not silently ignored; reconciliation state
is auditable; provider timeouts are not assumed successful; external
duplicate callbacks are ignored through idempotency.
