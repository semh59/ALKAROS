Split payment invariant
There is no SplitPayment value in payment_method. A split payment is
represented by:
Bill
 ├──  Payment #1 (Cash)     └──  PaymentAllocation
 ├──  Payment #2 (BankCard) └──  PaymentAllocation
 └──  Payment #3 (MealCard) └──  PaymentAllocation
The authoritative allocation relationship is payment_allocations.
payments.allocated_amount is a cached projection of its allocation rows.
