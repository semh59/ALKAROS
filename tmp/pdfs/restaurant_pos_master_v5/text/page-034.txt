PART II — DOMAIN SPECIFICATION
II.0 Purpose
This part defines the core domain model, invariants, state machines,
command boundaries, and operational rules for the restaurant POS
product. It is intentionally positioned before the database schema (Part III)
because the schema must follow domain rules, not the other way around.
The product is a single-branch restaurant POS with: local-first operation,
waiter PWA, cashier terminal, kitchen printers, Hugin T300 fiscal/payment
device, QNB eSolutions invoice integration, meal card settlement, QR
ordering, online ordering, daily menu + portion pool, customer accounts,
audit, backup, reconciliation.
Microservices are not part of the target architecture.
