I.26A Bill ↔  Payment Closure Invariant
Bill-level money fields have explicit ownership:
Bill.payable_amount = final amount that must be covered.
Bill.allocated_amount = SUM of valid
PaymentAllocation.allocated_amount for the Bill.
Bill.paid_amount = SUM of allocated amounts belonging to payments
that are in a financially final state (Approved and not subsequently
reversed/refunded).
Bill.change_amount = SUM of cash change actually returned for the
Bill; it is not part of the amount allocated to the Bill.
Payment.allocated_amount is a cached/derived operational value equal to
the SUM of that Payment's PaymentAllocation rows. It is never an
independently authoritative amount.
A Bill may become Paid only when:
paid_amount == payable_amount
AND all required payment allocations are valid
AND no required payment is Pending / Unknown / Declined / 
Cancelled
AND no unresolved fiscal/reconciliation condition blocks closure
For split payment, there is no SplitPayment tender. The Bill is closed from
the composition of its individual approved tenders. (Duplicate-allocation
protection at the database level: III.8.2, Corrections v5 §1.)
