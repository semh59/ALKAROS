II.3.7 Recipe & RecipeVersion
A recipe is the conceptual definition. A recipe version is the historical,
immutable operational definition used for a specific production batch or
cost basis. Recipe version must never be rewritten for historical accounting
purposes.
II.3.8 ProductionBatch
A concrete production event. Must always reference exactly one recipe
version — one of the most important immutability rules in the product.
II.3.9 PortionReservation
Temporary lock of a prepared portion for an order or channel. Not the same
as consumption.
II.3.10 MealCardSettlement
The operational accounting layer between the restaurant and meal card
provider. Not the same thing as customer current account.
II.3.11 CustomerAccount
The restaurant-side receivable relationship with a customer/company. Used
for periodic invoicing and deferred payment scenarios.
II.3.12 FiscalDocument
A provider/device-backed legal or operational fiscal artifact. Not the same
thing as bill or payment.
II.3.13 KitchenTicket
A production instruction set derived from one or more orders and routed by
station. Not a print job. Composed of KitchenTicketItem rows, each with
its own finer-grained state — see II.5.7A.
II.3.14 PrintJob
The physical printing task. A failed print job must not destroy the order or
the kitchen ticket.
