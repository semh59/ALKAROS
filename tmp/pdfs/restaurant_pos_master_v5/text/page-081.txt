timestamp, legal retention exceptions, financial history separated from
directly identifying customer data.
Customer PII tables MUST support: retention_until,
anonymization_status, anonymized_at (implemented — III.18.1, III.18.2).
deletion_requested_at is used where the product receives an applicable
deletion request.
Financial/legal records are not deleted merely because a customer is
anonymized: (1) identify the PII fields that may legally be removed, (2)
anonymize those fields, (3) retain required fiscal/accounting records, (4)
write an audit record, (5) make the operation idempotent.
Canonical status: Active, AnonymizationPending, Anonymized,
RetentionBlocked.
The schema must never rely on application comments alone for this
lifecycle; the fields and constraints must exist in the database model —
confirmed satisfied as of this merge.
III.35 Final schema boundary
Rules: do not over-normalize core transaction tables; do not denormalize
historical reference data that must remain stable; do not mix fiscal/provider
data directly into core order tables; do not encode UI state as source of
truth; do not use a single mega-table for all transaction types; do not
collapse bill/payment/fiscal into one record.
