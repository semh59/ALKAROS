II.6.13 Meal card vs customer account
(Renumbered from "§6.12" — Corrections v5 §8.1.)
Rules: meal card settlement is provider-facing; customer account is
customer-facing; they are not the same domain; they may both appear in
the same bill, but their accounting flows differ.
II.7 Channel rules
II.7.1 Cashier channel
direct POS interaction, table management, payment initiation, bill closure,
fiscal trigger.
II.7.2 Waiter channel
PWA on personal phone, table selection, order input, modifiers, notes,
submission, view status, no silent offline finalization unless explicitly
enabled in a later policy.
II.7.3 QR channel
public menu access, secure token, restaurant confirmation by default, no
direct local network exposure.
II.7.4 Online channel
provider adapter, webhook ingestion, catalog mapping, status sync,
cancellation handling, stock and availability reconciliation.
II.8 Kitchen rules
II.8.1 Kitchen ticket creation
Kitchen tickets are derived from accepted operational orders.
