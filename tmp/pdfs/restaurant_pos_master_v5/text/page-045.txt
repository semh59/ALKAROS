II.6.5 Bill ↔  Payment closure and overpayment
Money concepts are deliberately separated:
Bill.payable_amount: authoritative amount due.
Bill.allocated_amount: sum of valid PaymentAllocation rows.
Bill.paid_amount: sum of allocated amounts belonging to financially
final payments.
Bill.change_amount: sum of actual cash change returned.
Payment.allocated_amount: cached/derived sum of its
PaymentAllocation rows.
PaymentAllocation.allocated_amount: authoritative allocation record.
A Bill is Paid only when:
paid_amount == payable_amount
AND all required allocations are valid
AND no required payment remains Pending / Unknown / Declined / 
Cancelled
AND no blocking fiscal/reconciliation condition remains 
unresolved
SplitPayment is a composition pattern, not a tender type.
Bill payable: 950
Cash tendered: 1000
Payment allocated: 950
Change returned: 50
The payment event, allocation, and change are three different concepts.
II.6.6 Payment allocation integrity
PaymentAllocation is the authoritative bridge between a Payment and the
amount it covers on a Bill or BillAllocation. Rules: a Payment may have one
or more allocations; an allocation may cover a BillAllocation or a direct
payable segment; Payment.allocated_amount is derived from allocation
rows; Bill.allocated_amount is derived from all valid allocations; no
allocation may exceed the remaining payable amount of its target;
duplicate callbacks must not create duplicate allocation rows — enforced
via a partial unique index for the direct-to-Bill case (bill_allocation_id
IS NULL) plus a compound unique index for the BillAllocation-targeted
