III.38 Schema design non-negotiables
1. Do not use floating point for money.
2. Do not allow silent deletion of financial/fiscal/audit history.
3. Do not collapse order, bill, payment, and fiscal document into one table.
4. Do not put external provider payloads inside core business tables where
they would pollute the model.
5. Do not let recipe changes alter past production history.
6. Do not let QR bypass confirmation policy.
7. Do not let print failure destroy operational data.
8. Do not let payment timeout become silent success.
9. Do not let stock reservation go negative.
10. Do not let customer PII design ignore KVKK retention needs.
11. Do not let online provider IDs become internal primary keys.
12. Do not let meal card settlement and customer account merge into one
concept.
