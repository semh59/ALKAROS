    generated always as (
        case transaction_type
            when 'Charge'     then 'Debit'
            when 'Invoice'    then 'Debit'
            when 'Adjustment' then null   -- sign carried by 
`amount`
            when 'Payment'    then 'Credit'
            when 'Credit'     then 'Credit'
            when 'Debit'      then 'Debit'
            when 'Refund'     then 'Credit'
        end
    ) stored;
This guarantees direction can never contradict transaction_type; it
exists purely to simplify reporting queries (aging, balance calculation)
without a join or CASE in every consumer.
