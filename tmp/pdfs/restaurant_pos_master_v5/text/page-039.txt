II.3.16 Table (new subsection — closes prior audit gap B7)
The physical/operational representation of a seating unit. Table is not Bill,
not Order. A Table's current_status reflects seating availability, not
financial state. See II.5.15 for the formal state machine and its deliberately
lighter audit/reconciliation treatment relative to financial entities.
