Physical Print Retry Safeguards & Crash-Window Recovery Verification (PDF:I.16-I.20, PDF:II.5.7-II.5.8, V1-KIT-004):
1. Crash Window Uncertainty:
   - When socket crashes, timeouts, or network disconnects occur during byte transmission before physical ACK, status transitions to Unknown.
   - Prohibits automated retry: the system NEVER automatically retries a print in Unknown status, preventing duplicate food preparation in the kitchen.

2. Operator-Controlled Reprint Approval:
   - Deliveries in Unknown status are queued for operator visual review.
   - Operator can approve reprint (ApproveOperatorReprintAsync) or reject/dismiss reprint (RejectOperatorReprintAsync).

3. Distinct Reprint Watermark Banner:
   - Approved reprints generate distinct watermark headers:
     "*** TEKRAR BASKI / REPRINT ***"
     "*** MUKERRER RISKLI KOPYA ***"
     "ONAYLAYAN: {operatorId}"
     "NEDEN: {reason}"
   - Clearly alerts kitchen staff that the ticket is a duplicate copy.

4. Data Safety Invariant:
   - Failed or unknown print deliveries never modify, cancel, or delete Order or KitchenTicket records.

5. Database Migrations & Persistence:
   - 018-physical-print-recovery.up.sql / 018-physical-print-recovery.down.sql manages kitchen.physical_print_deliveries.
   - Cleanly tested and verified on PostgreSQL 18.
