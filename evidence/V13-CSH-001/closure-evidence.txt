## Post-closure fix (2026-09-17, same-day deep review)
A self-requested deep review of this session's work found a genuine race:
`OpenSessionAsync`'s `GetByTerminalIdAsync` check and its `AddAsync` insert
are not atomic, so two truly concurrent opens on the same terminal could
both pass the in-memory policy check and race to insert — the DB's partial
unique index (migration 122) still guarantees only one row survives, but
the losing caller previously saw a raw `PostgresException` instead of the
clean `ActiveCashSessionExistsException` a sequential second call gets.
Fixed in `CashSessionLifecycleService.OpenSessionAsync` by catching that
specific unique-violation (constraint `ux_cash_sessions_one_active_per_terminal`)
and translating it into the same domain exception. New test
`ConcurrentOpenSessionAsyncCallsOnTheSameTerminalNeverBothSucceed` drives
two real concurrent `OpenSessionAsync` calls via `Task.WhenAll` and asserts
exactly one wins — re-ran the full suite: 12/12 (was 11/11).
