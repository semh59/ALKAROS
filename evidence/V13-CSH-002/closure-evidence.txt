## Scope executed
Implements the append-only CashTransaction ledger and the expected/actual
close variance computation (docs/domain/cash-session-design.md §4-5,
PDF:I.38-I.44/II.2.7/II.5.9/III.9.2).

- `src/Modules/Cash/TransactionLedger/CashTransaction.cs`: immutable entry.
  `Amount` is always a positive magnitude; `Direction` (In/Out) carries the
  sign — Opening/Sale/CashIn are always In and CashOut/Refund are always
  Out (constructor refuses any other direction for those); CountAdjustment
  and ClosingDifference may legitimately go either way (a recount can be
  short or over; a closing variance can be a shortage or an overage), so
  their direction is caller-supplied. Payment linkage: Sale/Refund require
  a `RelatedPaymentId`, every other type must not carry one. Explicit
  correction requirement: CountAdjustment must always carry non-empty
  `Notes` stating why.
- `CashTransactionExceptions.cs`: InvalidCashTransactionAmountException,
  InvalidCashTransactionDirectionException, MissingRelatedPaymentException,
  UnexpectedRelatedPaymentException, MissingCountAdjustmentReasonException.
- `ICashTransactionLedgerRepository.cs` + `PostgresCashTransactionLedgerRepository.cs`:
  `RecordAsync` (insert-only, no update path exists anywhere — a correction
  is always a new row), `GetBySessionIdAsync` (bounded, MaxUnpagedRows
  idiom), and `ComputeExpectedCashAsync` — sums every entry's signed amount
  except CountAdjustment/ClosingDifference (the design doc's own formula:
  ExpectedCash = OpeningBalance + CashIn + Sales - CashOut - Refunds, with
  CountAdjustment/ClosingDifference being audit-trail entries recorded
  AFTER the fact, not inputs to that running total). Acceptance evidence
  ("cash reconstructed from immutable entries alone, difference recorded,
  never silently overwritten") is satisfied literally: Opening is itself
  just another ledger row, so ExpectedCash needs no external "opening
  balance" input at all — it is 100% reconstructed from the session's own
  rows, and every row is insert-only.
- `CashTransactionLedgerModule.cs`: a third `IModule` in the Cash assembly,
  kept separate from `CashModule` (V1-CSH-001) and
  `CashSessionLifecycleModule` (V13-CSH-001) so this task never touched
  either shared file.
- `database/migrations/V13/V13-CSH-002/124-cash-transactions.{up,down}.sql`:
  `cash.cash_transactions`, FK to `cash.cash_sessions` and (nullable) to
  `payments.payments`. Defense-in-depth CHECK constraints mirror all three
  application-level rules above (direction-matches-type,
  payment-linkage-matches-type, count-adjustment-requires-notes), plus
  amount > 0.
- `tests/Modules/Cash/TransactionLedger/**`: new test project. 10 pure
  constructor tests (no DB) + 12 real-Postgres tests: round trip, the
  design doc's own worked formula (500+20+100-15-5=600) computed purely
  from persisted ledger rows, confirms CountAdjustment/ClosingDifference
  are excluded from that sum, a zero-entry session sums to 0, and 5
  raw-SQL "database test" cases proving the CHECK/FK constraints reject
  the same violations the constructor already refuses even if a future
  caller bypasses it (same pattern as PostgresPaymentTests/
  PostgresPaymentAllocationRepositoryTests' own DatabaseRejects* tests).
